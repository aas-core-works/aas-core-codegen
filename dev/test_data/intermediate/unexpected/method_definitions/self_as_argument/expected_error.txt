The method 'is_same_as_itself' of the class 'Something' uses self other than to access its properties or methods, *e.g.*, it passes self as an argument, assigns it to a variable or checks it with isinstance. Some targets can not let the instance escape the member access, *e.g.*, ``this`` is a raw pointer in C++, while the instances are passed around as shared pointers. We do not support such uses of self at the moment; please contact the developers if you need this feature.
