We support iterating over the items of a dictionary, ``for k, v in x.items()``, only in a for-loop statement, but not in ``any(...)``. The targets transpile ``any(...)`` into lambdas, *e.g.*, LINQ in C# and streams in Java, and a lambda in C# or Java can not unpack a key-value pair into two variables. Please iterate over the keys and look the values up, *e.g.*, ``any(x[k] > 0 for k in x)``, or write a for-loop statement.
